# AquaVerify — 3-5 Minute Demo Video Script & Shot List
**OneAquaHealth × IEEE Hackathon | Track 1: Citizen Science UX**

---

## 📽️ Overview & Video Objectives
This document provides a structured recording script and shot-by-shot timeline for producing the official **AquaVerify** hackathon video submission. The video demonstrates how AquaVerify solves the challenge of complex stream observation tools by delivering a guided, beginner-friendly citizen science experience.

* **Target Duration**: 3 minutes 30 seconds
* **Target Audience**: Hackathon Evaluators, Environmental Educators, Citizen Science Coordinators

---

## ⏱️ Shot-by-Shot Timeline & Voiceover Script

### Scene 1: Introduction & The Problem (0:00 - 0:45)
* **Visual**: Opening title slide with AquaVerify logo and tagline: *"Observe water. Understand your surroundings."* Cut to split-screen showing complicated scientific water monitoring forms vs. clean AquaVerify interface.
* **Voiceover Script**:
  > *"Freshwater monitoring is crucial for healthy communities, but traditional citizen science tools often fail beginners. Technical jargon like 'turbidity NTU values' or confusing forms discourage students and local volunteers from making repeated observations. Welcome to **AquaVerify** — built for the OneAquaHealth × IEEE Hackathon under Track 1: Citizen Science UX. AquaVerify simplifies freshwater observations into a guided, intuitive experience that anyone can use."*

---

### Scene 2: Welcome Splash & Home Dashboard (0:45 - 1:30)
* **Visual**: App opens on Welcome Screen. User taps *"Get Started"*, arriving at the Home Dashboard. Highlight the total observation counter, recent observation previews, and beginner tips card.
* **Voiceover Script**:
  > *"When a user launches AquaVerify, they are greeted with a clear, calm visual identity inspired by natural water bodies. The Home Dashboard serves as the user's personal observation workspace, showing their total recorded contributions, recent activity, and daily citizen science tips."*

---

### Scene 3: The Guided Observation Form Wizard (1:30 - 2:30)
* **Visual**: User taps *"Start New Observation"*. Screen transitions through the 4-step wizard:
  1. Entering title ("Greenpark River Check") and choosing water body type ("River").
  2. Selecting water clarity ("Clear") and visible colour ("Normal/natural"). Tapping the expandable *"Why we ask this"* tooltip card.
  3. Recording odour ("No unusual odour"), surface movement ("Light movement"), and visible litter.
  4. Adding field notes ("Clean water flowing near footbridge. Ducks visible.").
* **Voiceover Script**:
  > *"Instead of intimidating users with a wall of fields, AquaVerify breaks the form into four guided steps. Technical terms are replaced with plain-language questions. For example, instead of asking for turbidity measurements, we ask 'How clear does the water look?'. Expandable educational cards explain the ecological context behind each metric, helping citizens learn while observing. Plus, an 'Unsure' option is always available to ensure honest data collection."*

---

### Scene 4: Review, Save & Local Storage Persistence (2:30 - 3:00)
* **Visual**: User arrives at the Review & Save screen. Highlight the summary cards, edit options, and the prominent **Non-Scientific Disclaimer banner**. User taps *"Save Observation"*. Success modal appears, navigating to History.
* **Voiceover Script**:
  > *"Before saving, users can review all recorded information. AquaVerify maintains transparency by explicitly noting that entries represent citizen field observations rather than lab-certified tests. Tapping 'Save Observation' persists the data locally on the device instantly using SharedPreferences — operating 100% offline without needing cloud login."*

---

### Scene 5: History, Details & Re-Engagement (3:00 - 3:30)
* **Visual**: Show the History Screen with search bar filtering entries by keyword or water body chip. Tap the newly created observation to open Details Screen. Highlight the *"Copy Summary"* export button and *"Make Another Observation"* CTA.
* **Voiceover Script**:
  > *"In the History screen, users can search, filter, or delete saved records. The Details screen provides a complete report readout and lets users copy a formatted summary to share with local watershed groups. Prominent CTAs encourage users to continue observing nearby streams. Thank you for exploring AquaVerify!"*
